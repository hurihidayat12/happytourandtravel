Lombok 3D2N Tour Package

Snorkeling Pink Beach • Waterfall Adventure • Sasak Mandalika Cultural Tour

Explore the Beauty of Lombok

Experience a perfect 3-day journey exploring Lombok’s breathtaking nature from the pink sand beaches and underwater life of Gili Petelu to majestic waterfalls at Mount Rinjani’s foothills, and the rich Sasak culture at Mandalika.

Highlights

• Snorkeling at Pink Beach
• Visit to Tiu Kelep / Benang Kelambu Waterfall
• Explore Sasak Traditional Villages
• Stunning views at Merese Hill & Tanjung Aan Beach

Tour Itinerary

Day 1 – Pick Up & Pink Beach Snorkeling

Welcome at Lombok Airport with Sesek Shawl. Visit Sukarara Village, then head to Tanjung Luar Port for a snorkeling trip at Gili Petelu or Gili Gambir.

  • Snorkeling at Pink Beach 1 & 2
  • Picnic-style lunch at Pink Beach
  • Pasir Island (Sand Island)
  • Dinner & Hotel Check-in

Day 2 – Waterfall Tour (2 Options)

Option 1: Sendang Gile & Tiu Kelep Waterfalls, Senaru Village.
Option 2: Benang Stokel & Benang Kelambu Waterfalls, Rinjani Geopark.

  • Local lunch & dinner
  • Return to Mataram, free program

Day 3 – Sasak Mandalika Tour & Airport Transfer

  • Visit souvenir shops in Mataram
  • Explore Sade/Ende Traditional Village
  • Enjoy views from Bukit Seger & Merese Hill
  • Drop-off at Airport

Detailed Tour Itinerary

Day 1 – Pick Up at Airport & Pink Beach Snorkeling (Lunch & Dinner)

  • 🌿 Warm welcome with Sesek Shawl Garland (traditional Sasak woven cloth)
  • 🌿 Visit Sukarara Weaving Village – center of songket and Sasak traditional crafts
  • 🌿 Transfer to Tanjung Luar / Elong-Elong Port
  • 🌿 Snorkeling Trip to Gili Petelu or Gili Gambir
  • 🌿 Stop at Pink Beach 1 – enjoy a picnic-style lunch with ocean views
  • 🌿 Continue to Pink Beach 2 – unspoiled and naturally pink
  • 🌿 Visit Sand Island – a sandbar appearing only at low tide
  • 🌿 Dinner & hotel check-in

Day 2 – Waterfall Adventure (Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner)

Option 1 – Sendang Gile & Tiu Kelep Waterfalls

  • 🌿 Visit Senaru Village at the foot of Mount Rinjani
  • 🌿 Explore Sendang Gile Waterfall – single fall ~100m high
  • 🌿 Continue to Tiu Kelep Waterfall – scenic & refreshing
  • 🌿 Lunch at a local restaurant
  • 🌿 Return to Mataram City, dinner & free evening

Option 2 – Benang Stokel & Benang Kelambu Waterfalls

  • 🌿 Visit Benang Stokel Village in Rinjani Geopark
  • 🌿 Explore Benang Stokel and Benang Kelambu waterfalls
  • 🌿 Lunch at local restaurant, dinner at Mataram, back to hotel

Day 3 – Sasak Mandalika Tour & Airport Transfer (Breakfast)

  • 🌿 Breakfast at hotel
  • 🌿 City tour in Mataram visiting Lombok souvenir shops
  • 🌿 Visit Sade/Ende Traditional Village – authentic Sasak culture
  • 🌿 Lunch excluded
  • 🌿 Enjoy views from Bukit Seger & Mandalika Circuit
  • 🌿 Relax at Tanjung Aan Beach & Merese Hill
  • 🌿 Airport transfer
